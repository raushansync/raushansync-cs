Data Science

RaushanSYNC Data Science is designed to help learners understand data as a source of evidence and insight — not just as something to be processed with libraries or tools. The emphasis is on foundations: how data is generated, collected, represented, explored, and interpreted; how uncertainty is quantified using probability and statistics; and how models are built, evaluated, and improved. From exploratory analysis and statistical reasoning to machine learning and responsible deployment, the structure focuses on developing sound judgment, reproducible thinking, and the ability to reason from data — skills that remain valuable beyond any specific software or workflow.
