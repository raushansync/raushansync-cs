RaushanSYNC CS & DS

Hi, I'm Raushan.

I created this website because I believe Computer Science and Data Science should be learned from first principles, not by memorising syntax, frameworks, interview questions, or tutorials. Behind every program, algorithm, and model is a simple idea waiting to be understood, and my aim is to help you discover those ideas.

Here you'll find carefully designed articles, video lessons, coding projects, and practice exercises that focus on building genuine understanding. Whether we're learning programming, data structures, algorithms, databases, web development, or machine learning, I want you to understand not only how something works, but why it works.

This website is designed for curious learners, from students beginning their journey in Class 11 and Class 12 to college students and self-learners who want to build strong foundations. I believe the best programmers are not the ones who memorise the most code, but those who understand the principles behind computation and can apply them to solve real-world problems.

I'm currently pursuing a BS in Data Science and Applications at IIT Madras, and building RaushanSYNC has become one of my ways of learning. Every lesson I write encourages me to study more deeply, question my own understanding, and explain difficult concepts as simply and clearly as possible. As this website grows, I hope we can continue learning together.

Thank you for visiting. I hope these lessons inspire you to stay curious, think computationally, and develop the confidence to build, analyze, and solve meaningful problems through Computer Science and Data Science.
