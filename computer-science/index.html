Computer Science

RaushanSYNC Computer Science is designed to help learners understand computing as a system of ideas — not just a collection of languages, tools, or syntax. The focus is on building concepts from first principles: how programs execute, how data is represented and organized, how algorithms are designed and analyzed, and how abstraction enables scale and efficiency. From foundational programming to data structures, algorithms, and computational thinking, the structure is meant not only for academic study but to develop deep, transferable problem-solving skills that remain valuable beyond any specific technology or framework.
